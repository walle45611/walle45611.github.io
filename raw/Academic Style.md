- Contextualisation: do writers overtly [introduce their interpretation](https://lifelong-learning.ox.ac.uk/arguing/), or do they embed it within a broader discussion or debate (where other scholarship is cited)?
- [Writer Visibility](https://lifelong-learning.ox.ac.uk/writer-visibility-use-of-personal-pronouns-passive-proxy-nouns/): are personal pronouns (I or we) used, or do writers opt for passive or impersonal noun constructions?
- [Hedging language](https://lifelong-learning.ox.ac.uk/hedging/): how cautious or confident are writers in putting forward novel interpretation? What hedging constructions, or signals of confidence, are they using?
- Supporting the Interpretations: what strategies do writers use to substantiate their own interpretation? Do they regularly appeal to [data](https://lifelong-learning.ox.ac.uk/presenting-results/), other [scholarly works/interpretation](https://lifelong-learning.ox.ac.uk/incorporating-scholarship-into-your-writing/), or does it suffice to refer to logical argumentations
