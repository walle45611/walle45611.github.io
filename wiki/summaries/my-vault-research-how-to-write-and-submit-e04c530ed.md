## Source Notes

- The introduction should provide only a brief overview of the whole paper. It should not give too many details.
- It should include the topic, motivation, problem, rationale, purpose, research questions or objectives, method or approach, and a brief outline of the paper structure.
- The introduction is usually written toward the end of the writing process, like the abstract.
